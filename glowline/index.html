What Glowline can and can't do

  • System audio is everything your default output device plays. Glowline can tell whether Spotify is playing, but it can't separate Spotify's sound from other apps.
  • Cover art, track info and the play/next/previous buttons come from Windows' media controls (the same source as the volume flyout). No Spotify API or account is used.
  • The glow covers the displays you tick. It doesn't change monitor brightness or hardware settings, and it can't draw over exclusive-fullscreen games, the lock screen or secure Windows prompts.
  • In a browser tab the light only appears inside this page or its full-screen preview. Install the desktop app for cover art, playback controls, the screen glow and Spotify detection.
  • In Chrome or Edge on Windows, "Listen" asks you to share a screen. Tick "Also share system audio" in that dialog.
  • Screen and audio capture always need your explicit, visible permission, and you can stop them at any time.
